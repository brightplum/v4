What the DonorBoost Audit Does

The DonorBoost audit identifies visible risks, gaps, and opportunities across your donor journey — from first discovery to long-term retention. 

It answers questions like:

  • Where does our donor funnel break down?
  • What signals are funders and donors seeing today?
  • What are we missing that peers are doing better?

How this Audit is created

DonorBoost is built using a structured evaluation framework developed by Bright Plum and informed by nonprofit fundraising best practices. This audit is designed to surface what needs attention. DonorBoost Plus is where we determine how to fix it, in what order, and with what expected return.

Each audit includes:

  • Review of publicly available data (website, donation UX, filings, social presence, third-party sources)
  • Donor journey and funnel analysis
  • Risk and opportunity synthesis

AI tools are used to accelerate analysis and synthesis, but all findings are reviewed, refined, and finalized by Bright Plum. If you need internal data analysis, interviews, or execution planning, that’s where DonorBoost Plus comes in.

DonorBoost Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from a traditional fundraising audit?

DonorBoost focuses on decision clarity, not just diagnostics. It identifies where your donor journey breaks down and prioritizes what will have the biggest revenue impact first, rather than overwhelming you with every possible issue.

Is the free DonorBoost audit automated or AI-generated?

DonorBoost uses a structured evaluation framework informed by nonprofit fundraising best practices. AI tools assist with synthesis, but all findings are reviewed, refined, and finalized by Bright Plum. This is not a generic or templated report.

What information do you need from us for the free audit?

None. The free DonorBoost audit is based entirely on publicly available information, including your website, donation experience, public filings, and digital presence.

How long does it take to receive the free audit?

Most organizations receive their DonorBoost audit within 2-4 business days, depending on current demand.

Will this audit tell us exactly how to fix our fundraising?

The free audit identifies where and why issues exist and recommends priorities. DonorBoost Plus provides the detailed strategy, messaging, and execution roadmap required to implement solutions.

Who should review this audit internally?

DonorBoost is designed for Executive Directors, Development Leads, and Boards. Many organizations share the audit during leadership or board meetings to align on priorities.

What if our organization isn’t ready for DonorBoost Plus yet?

That’s common. Many organizations start with the free audit to build clarity and internal alignment, then upgrade when timing and resources allow.

Does DonorBoost Plus include implementation support?

DonorBoost Plus delivers strategy, messaging, and a 12-month execution roadmap. Ongoing implementation support can be scoped separately based on your needs.
