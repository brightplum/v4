The problem

There's always something wrong. You just don't always know it yet.

Your team keeps the communications running, the enrollment pages live, the event calendars updated, and the department sites from embarrassing anyone. You do this with two or three people, a backlog that never gets shorter, and a CMS that became your problem when the person who understood it left for a private-sector salary.

The accessibility audit arrived six weeks before the semester started. A security patch was three versions behind and nobody noticed until it mattered. Something broke the night before a major announcement and the agency took 48 hours to respond. The last redesign was done by a shop that has since been acquired, handed off twice, and now doesn't return emails.

You're not behind because you're disorganized. You're behind because the scope of what a small higher-ed marketing team is responsible for has grown faster than the headcount ever did. What you need isn't another ticket system. It's a team that already knows your site and tells you what needs to happen before you have to ask.

How it works

Every engagement begins with a Website Risk Sprint.

Before we recommend a plan, we run a fixed-scope, fixed-price sprint across your sites. We work through the full playbook, score you on the six dimensions that decide whether your website is an asset or a liability, and hand you a prioritized roadmap of what needs attention and when. You keep that picture whether or not you continue with us.

If you do continue, the sprint shows which level of support actually fits — so your plan is based on evidence instead of a guess. Your sprint fee is credited toward your first month. The sprint is the diagnosis. The plans below are the treatment.
