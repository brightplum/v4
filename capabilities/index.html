Proof

Past performance

U.S. Department of Justice, Office of Justice Programs (OJP)

Partnered with General Dynamics Information Technology (GDIT) on Acquia Drupal architecture, backend and frontend development, and website quality support across high-profile justice websites including CrimeSolutions.gov and BJS.gov. Completed 150+ tasks at a 99% completion rate, including 18 API development and integration projects (OAuth, JSON API endpoints, custom data integrations), UI and content-management enhancements, advanced search, and 75+ bug fixes and performance optimizations.

Department of Defense Education Activity (DoDEA)

Partnered with Promet Solutions on a multi-part Drupal engagement: 50+ tasks spanning Acquia Drupal data migrations, custom frontend development, and content management. Built taxonomies and organizational data structures, specialized webforms, school-specific handbook layouts, and SEO enhancements including XML sitemaps, plus Acquia Site Studio training and system architecture support.

Department of Homeland Security (DHS), HSIN

Partnered with MetroStar on the Homeland Security Information Network (HSIN) Tech Challenge, focused on incident management and emergency response. Built JSON:API integrations to expose incident data, OAuth authentication and custom entity development for secure access, incident-form development with automated alert status updates, and PHPUnit testing frameworks, all built to federal security standards.

New Jersey State Courts

Delivered technical support and system maintenance for the judicial platform: 70+ tasks, including 46 critical bug resolutions across file management, press-release automation, and content types. Implemented role- and deletion-protection safeguards for sensitive judicial data and optimized search for legal documents, Supreme Court appeals, and the Rules of Court.

City of Baltimore, Maryland

Partnered with XCell to modernize the city's web presence: 175+ tasks and 48 custom content-management components. Implemented Elasticsearch and Swiftype multilingual search, 311 service-request functionality, department- and city-wide alert systems, WCAG accessibility compliance, and secure department-level user management.
